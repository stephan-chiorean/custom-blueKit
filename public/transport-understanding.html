Semantic Telemetry

We spent decades optimizing how systems transport telemetry. The next step is transporting understanding.

Today's pipeline defers understanding until the very end — after the logs, after the storage, after the dashboards. Everything upstream is deliberately semantics-agnostic. Move understanding into the pipeline and every downstream consumer starts from meaning instead of raw evidence.

Pipelines today aren't opaque — they're deliberately semantics-agnostic. OpenTelemetry, Kafka and Datadog are designed to move arbitrary events reliably without imposing meaning. That neutrality is a feature for general-purpose infrastructure. It is also why understanding never accumulates.
