02 · What a grant looks like

Policy becomes a ceiling, not a prompt.

Instead of a rule per tool call, the policy describes an environment: who may enter, what lives inside, what data class it operates on, where output may land, and how long authority lasts.

The mechanism already exists and runs at planetary scale. This is AssumeRole with a session policy: a pre-registered ceiling, a per-instance narrowing, and a TTL. Effective permission is the intersection — which is precisely why a machine can be trusted to assemble the narrowing. The worst case is a context that's too small, never one that's too powerful.

03 · The part that changes everything

No unit of real work is the size of a session.

The chat session is ephemeral, private, and amnesiac — and it maps to nothing. An incident spans three days, four people, and a dozen sessions. A migration spans a quarter. The session is an artifact of how chat interfaces were built, not of how work is shaped.

A context that outlives the session fixes the mismatch. Sessions become what they actually are: activations of something longer-lived.

Durable context, ephemeral credentials. A three-day context doesn't mean three-day tokens. Identity, state, taint, and ceiling persist; credentials are re-minted per activation and die with it. A suspended context holds no live authority at all — which makes "reopen" a free re-authorization checkpoint, and makes revocation as simple as marking the object instead of chasing tokens.

04 · Contamination as a property, not a verdict

Per-call detection has no memory.

A stateless classifier sees one payload, decides, and forgets. It cannot know that a poisoned document entered on Tuesday and shaped a pull request on Thursday. The causal link exists in the world and nowhere in the system. A durable context makes contamination an accruing attribute — labels only ever go up.

This is the exact shape of a compromised dependency and an SBOM: which builds included it? The context is the SBOM of agentic work. Without a durable object, the honest answer to "what did the poisoned source affect" is we don't know — permanently.

Monotonic labels have a known failure mode: eventually everything is red. Information-flow control already named the release valve — declassification, an explicit, privileged, recorded act of lowering a label. Rare enough to mean something, attributable when it happens.

05 · Composition

Two directions, both enforced by construction.

Contexts nest. A feature spawns a test run; an incident spawns a rollback; any of them can spawn a sub-agent. The rule falls straight out of the intersection semantics — nobody has to remember to write a check.

Incident ctx_4821
ceiling C · taint T
└ Rollback ctx_4822 (sub-agent)
ceiling ⊆ C · taint ⊇ T
↓ capabilities intersect downward ↑ taint propagates upward
Delegation cannot escalate.
Contamination cannot hide.

A child can never exceed its parent's ceiling, so handing work to a sub-agent is safe by construction rather than by review. But contamination found in a child dirties the parent, because the parent consumed its output. Together, these make multi-agent delegation tractable instead of terrifying.

06 · The exceptions, defined properly

High-consequence has an actual definition.

A hand-maintained list of scary verbs doesn't scale and can't be audited. Treat the context as a long-running transaction, and the rule derives itself:

Rule

An action is high-consequence when it escapes the context's ability to retract it.

This also argues for staged sinks: artifacts land in a holding area owned by the context and commit at close. A context found tainted at minute 40 retracts what it hasn't committed, instead of chasing published side effects across four systems.

09 · The claim worth defending
The unit of authorization, memory, and audit is the Context — and it outlives the session.

Admission establishes the operating environment. Ordinary actions proceed without interruption. Only actions that escape retraction require additional approval. The object persists, accrues provenance, and can be handed off, observed, attached to, and contained after the fact.

One caution

Don't claim "no approvals ever," and don't claim this replaces information-flow control. The defensible position is narrower and stronger: the default unit is the Context, flow labels ride inside it, and the gateway stays underneath for the adversary that assembly can't stop.
